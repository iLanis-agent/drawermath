Drawer math

A drawer that binds, a front that rubs, a stack that wastes its cabinet - all bought by eye. Drawer math turns the opening depth and a labeled set-back into the box and slide space, turns the opening size and a labeled gap into the front size, and turns the cabinet height and drawer count into equal front heights. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Slide plan - the opening depth minus a labeled set-back becomes the room for the box and slides.
  • Front plan - the opening width and height minus a labeled gap each side become the front size.
  • Stack plan - the cabinet height minus labeled gaps, shared by the drawer count, becomes equal front heights.

Labeled norms

Labeled norms: a 25 mm set-back behind the box, a 3 mm gap around each front - all illustrative defaults you can change. Openings are never perfectly square and hardware makers rate their own slides. This is planning arithmetic, not joinery instruction: measure the real opening and check the slide maker's sheet.
